[0] SUMMARY OF RECORD
  Gross edge is real: +0.0124/trade, 95% CI [+0.0062, +0.0178], excludes zero.
  Net edge at the realistic 1x cost band is approximately zero: +0.0002 (base fee) / -0.0006 (upper fee). At 2x it is negative (-0.0063).
  Calibration: beta moved from 1.165 (in-sample) to 1.077 (OOS), CIs overlapping only in a narrow band [1.116, 1.133] (width 0.017). The OOS CI [1.027, 1.133] still excludes 1.0.

  Of the four outcomes named in advance (§3): persists and tradeable / persists but not net of
  costs / decayed / never robust -- this combination (statistically significant gross edge,
  breakeven-ish net edge, beta still excluding 1.0 OOS but smaller than in-sample) is READ HERE
  as 'persists but not net of costs, with partial decay.' Not 'never robust' (gross edge is
  significant); not fully 'decayed' (OOS beta still excludes 1.0); not 'tradeable' (net edge at
  the realistic band does not clear zero with any margin).

  Break-even = 1.03x the observed 1x spread band. This is not a near-miss -- it is a measurement
  of how precisely this market prices its own frictions: the gross mispricing and the cost of
  capturing it are approximately the same size. That is the limits-to-arbitrage signature, and
  it is this project's actual finding, not an inconclusive result.

  Adversarial-review clause (§3): a LARGE POSITIVE net edge (net of costs, at a realistic band)
  would have required an adversarial pipeline review before any celebration, since free money in
  a public market is more likely a bug or a look-ahead leak than a genuine inefficiency. That did
  NOT happen here -- no band shows a large positive net edge. Stating this explicitly so the
  safeguard reads as correctly not invoked, not forgotten.

  What this result does NOT establish:
  - This is one frozen rule with fixed thresholds, not a search over rules. A different entry
    band, a liquidity filter, or a category-conditional rule might behave differently -- untested
    here by design, since testing them would have required the tuning freedom the
    pre-registration deliberately gave up.
  - The spread assumption is contemporary live books applied retroactively (W4a; Spearman +0.391
    ordinal support -- modest, not strong). Break-even sits at 1.03x the assumed band, close
    enough that a materially different true spread would flip the sign of the net edge. That is
    exactly why break-even is reported as the headline number rather than the net edge itself.
  - Six months of OOS on one venue. Not a claim about prediction markets generally, and not a
    claim about periods under a different fee regime than the one investigated in W4a.
